# Sample Singapore Itineraries

## Classic 3-Day Sightseeing Itinerary

**Day 1 — Marina Bay and Civic District (mostly outdoor, with indoor options)**
Morning: Gardens by the Bay, including the Cloud Forest and Flower Dome conservatories (indoor).
Afternoon: Marina Bay Sands observation deck; walk along the Marina Bay waterfront to the Merlion.
Evening: Civic District for dinner near the Singapore River; catch the Marina Bay Sands light show.

**Day 2 — Culture and Heritage (mixed indoor/outdoor)**
Morning: Chinatown — Buddha Tooth Relic Temple and Sri Mariamman Temple, browse the street markets.
Lunch: Hawker food at Chinatown Complex or Maxwell Food Centre.
Afternoon: Kampong Glam — Sultan Mosque, Haji Lane and Arab Street.
Evening: Little India for dinner and a walk along Serangoon Road.

**Day 3 — Sentosa Island (mostly outdoor, with major indoor attractions)**
Full day: Sentosa Island — beaches, S.E.A. Aquarium (indoor), Universal Studios Singapore (mixed
indoor/outdoor). This is designed as a single full-day, self-contained itinerary since Sentosa is a
separate island with its own transport.

## Family-with-Children Itinerary (3 Days)

**Day 1**: Gardens by the Bay conservatories (indoor, air-conditioned, stroller-friendly) in the
morning; Merlion Park photo stop; early dinner near Marina Bay.
**Day 2**: Mandai Wildlife Reserve — Singapore Zoo in the morning, Bird Paradise in the afternoon;
consider the Night Safari as an evening add-on for older children.
**Day 3**: Sentosa Island — S.E.A. Aquarium and the island's beaches; Universal Studios Singapore if
children are old enough for the rides.

Family-friendly notes: Gardens by the Bay, Bird Paradise, and S.E.A. Aquarium are all substantially
indoor or covered, making them reliable choices if rain is forecast.

## Cultural-Focus Itinerary (3 Days)
**Day 1**: Civic District — National Gallery Singapore, Asian Civilisations Museum, Raffles Hotel area
(all indoor or covered, good for a rainy day).
**Day 2**: Chinatown in the morning, Kampong Glam in the afternoon — temples, mosque, heritage
shophouses.
**Day 3**: Little India in the morning, Katong-Joo Chiat in the afternoon for Peranakan heritage and
food.

## Indoor-Heavy Itinerary (for Rainy Days)
Gardens by the Bay conservatories, ArtScience Museum, National Gallery Singapore, Asian Civilisations
Museum, S.E.A. Aquarium, Bird Paradise (largely covered walkways), and mall-based food halls are all
strong indoor or mostly-covered options that can substitute for outdoor activities when rain is in the
forecast.

## Outdoor-Heavy Itinerary (for Clear Weather)
Singapore Botanic Gardens, Southern Ridges walk (including Henderson Waves), Sentosa beaches,
Merlion Park and the Marina Bay waterfront promenade, and Pulau Ubin (a rustic offshore island reached
by bumboat) are best enjoyed in clear, dry conditions.

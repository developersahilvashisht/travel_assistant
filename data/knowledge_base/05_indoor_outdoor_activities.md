# Singapore Indoor and Outdoor Activities

This guide classifies major Singapore activities by whether they are primarily indoor (weatherproof),
primarily outdoor (weather-dependent), or mixed, to support weather-aware itinerary planning.

## Primarily Indoor (Good for Rain or Extreme Heat)
- Gardens by the Bay conservatories (Cloud Forest, Flower Dome)
- ArtScience Museum
- National Gallery Singapore
- Asian Civilisations Museum
- S.E.A. Aquarium
- Bird Paradise (largely covered walkways and aviaries)
- Shopping malls along Orchard Road
- Hawker centres and food halls located in covered complexes
- Museums in the Civic District generally

## Primarily Outdoor (Weather-Dependent)
- Merlion Park and the Marina Bay waterfront promenade
- Singapore Botanic Gardens
- Southern Ridges walk and Henderson Waves
- Bukit Timah Nature Reserve
- Sentosa beaches
- Pulau Ubin (rustic outdoor island, cycling and nature trails)
- Street-level exploration of Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam (though individual temples/mosques
  within these districts are indoor)

## Mixed Indoor/Outdoor
- Sentosa Island as a whole (beaches and outdoor attractions alongside indoor ones like S.E.A. Aquarium)
- Singapore Zoo and Night Safari (mostly outdoor walking paths, but many viewing areas are covered)
- Mandai Wildlife Reserve generally
- Universal Studios Singapore (mix of indoor rides and outdoor queues/walkways)

## Weather-Aware Planning Guidance
Because Singapore can have sudden, heavy but usually short downpours at any time of year, a practical
approach is to schedule outdoor, high-value activities (e.g. Gardens by the Bay's outdoor Supertree
Grove, Botanic Gardens, Sentosa beaches) for the part of the day the forecast shows as driest, and to
keep an indoor alternative (a nearby museum, mall, or the indoor conservatories) ready to swap in if
rain is expected. Evening outdoor activities (e.g. the Marina Bay light show, night walks) are
generally less affected by daytime rain forecasts.
